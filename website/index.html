About Me

I, Bradley Tanaka Makanjera, grew up in Sizinda, Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, a community that has played an important part in shaping my background and perspective on life.

Sizinda has historical roots as a residential township developed to accommodate railway workers during Bulawayo's industrial growth. Its history is connected to the city's working-class communities and development.

Growing up in Sizinda also means coming from a community that has faced significant social challenges—drug and substance abuse, youth violence, crime and difficult socioeconomic conditions. In April 2026 local reports documented residents organising responses to violence associated with drug and substance abuse, including acquiring vehicles to support community safety initiatives.

My background is connected to an environment where social challenges are a real concern, and where the circumstances surrounding young people can be difficult. I want my story to acknowledge where I come from without allowing those challenges to define who I am or determine what I can become.

Sizinda is where my story began, but it does not have to define where my story ends.
